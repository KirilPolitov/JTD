# JTD
Tower defense

# Enemy types:
normal - green plane, has 1 hp 1 currency upon defeat 1 life on leaking  
tank - gray plane has 5 hp 3 currency upon defeat 5 lives on leaking

# Tower types:
turret - costs 5 currency, deals 1 damage does not pierce  
missile launcher - costs 10 currency, deals 1 damage and splash damage of 1 damage to all in the area, projectiles move slower than those of the turret but have a larger hitbox, also has more range, attacks slower

# Controls:
select turret - Num1  
select missile launcher - Num2  
exit selection - Escape  
start next wave - Space  
select placed tower - left click on them  
place tower - left click while holding unplaced tower

# Cheats:
Money cheat - left alt + g + m + s
